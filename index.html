Featured · Beta · SaaS

FilmKit

Studio management for video production teams who are currently duct-taping Drive, email and spreadsheets together.

Delivery, revisions, client sign-off and invoicing usually live in four different places, and the handoff between them is where a production loses a week. FilmKit puts the whole chain in one place so a client's "can we see v3 again?" doesn't turn into an archaeology dig through a shared inbox.

  • Delivery & revisions — versions stay attached to the job instead of the thread they were emailed in.
  • Client sign-off — approval is a recorded action, not someone saying "yep looks good" on a call.
  • Invoicing — billing hangs off the same job record, so it doesn't get re-typed.
  • SaaS
  • Web app
  • Solo build

TrainyTracker

Real-time train tracking across NSW & Victoria, built for the phone that's already in your hand on the platform.

Live positions, platform info and timings. It's an installable PWA rather than a store app because the moment you need it is the moment you're standing on a cold platform wondering whether the 7:42 exists — that shouldn't require a download.

  • Live positions — where the train is now, not where the timetable says it should be.
  • Platform & timings — the two things you actually check before you run.
  • Installable — add to home screen, works like an app, no store listing.
  • PWA
  • Realtime data
  • NSW + VIC

Before you email me

What does it cost?

There's no price list here, because I'd be making it up before I know what the job is. You tell me what's wrong, I diagnose it, and you get scope and cost in plain English before I start. If the answer is "this is a five-minute fix, don't pay me for it," I'll say that too.

Do you work remote or on-site?

Both. I'm in Sydney, so on-site is straightforward around the city. Most reporting and automation work happens remotely anyway.

What happens if it breaks again?

I fix it. That's the short answer. The longer one is that I leave notes on what I changed and I'm still reachable after the invoice is sent — a job isn't finished the moment I get paid.

I don't know what's actually wrong. Is that a problem?

No — that's most of the job. "It's slow", "it keeps doing the thing", or "someone set this up in 2019 and left" are all fine starting points. Working out what's actually happening is the part you're hiring me for.
