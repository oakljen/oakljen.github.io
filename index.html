Troubleshooting & support

Slow computers, printers that won't cooperate, Wi-Fi dead zones, "why does this keep happening." The stuff that eats an hour here and there.

Automation & reporting

If you're copy-pasting numbers into a spreadsheet every week, that's a job for a script. I build Power BI dashboards and Power Automate/Python flows that do it for you.

Networks & setup

New office, new site, or a home network held together with hope. I'll design and set it up properly, with documentation so the next person isn't guessing.

Backups & infrastructure

If your backup plan is "it's never happened before," that's worth changing. Self-hosted or cloud, set up once and left running in the background.
