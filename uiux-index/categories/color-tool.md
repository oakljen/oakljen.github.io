# color-tool

### [0to255](https://0to255.com/)
- tags: tint-shade-generator
- quick tint/shade ramp for one color, useful for hover/active state variants

### [Accessible Colors](https://accessible-colors.com/)
- tags: wcag-contrast, suggest-fix
- checks a foreground/background pair against WCAG and suggests the nearest passing adjustment

### [Adobe Color](https://color.adobe.com/)
- tags: color-wheel, extract-from-image, harmony-rules
- color wheel + harmony-rule palette builder, can extract a palette from an uploaded image

### [Color Hunt](https://colorhunt.co/)
- tags: palette-gallery, trending
- trending community-submitted 4-color palettes

### [Colorable](https://colorable.jxnblk.com/)
- tags: contrast-grid, palette-wide-check
- contrast-ratio grid across an entire palette at once, not just one pair at a time

### [ColorBox](https://colorbox.io/)
- tags: lyft, systematic-scale, export
- Lyft's systematic color-scale builder for multi-hue design-system palettes, exports as data/CSS

### [Colormind](http://colormind.io/)
- tags: ai-generator, photo-extraction
- AI-trained palette generator, can also extract a palette from a photo

### [ColorSpace](https://mycolor.space/)
- tags: single-color-input, palette-generator
- generates full palettes (gradient, scale, shades) from one input color

### [Coolors](https://coolors.co/)
- tags: palette-generator
- fast palette generation tool, not a codebase

### [Huemint](https://huemint.com/)
- tags: ai-generator, brand-palette
- generative palette tool for brand/UI/print combos, weighted role sliders

### [Khroma](https://www.khroma.co/)
- tags: ai-personalized, trained-on-picks
- AI trained on 50 of your color picks, then generates endless palettes matching your taste

### [Leonardo](https://leonardocolor.io/)
- tags: contrast, accessible-scale, adobe
- Adobe's contrast-based accessible color scale generator, outputs perceptually-tuned ramps

### [Paletton](https://paletton.com/)
- tags: color-wheel, scheme-designer
- color-wheel scheme designer (mono/complementary/triad/tetrad) with live mock-UI preview

### [Radix Colors](https://www.radix-ui.com/colors)
- tags: 12-step-scale, accessible, generator
- 12-step accessible color scale generator/model; all 30 hues vendored at component-source/radix-colors

### [Realtime Colors](https://www.realtimecolors.com/)
- tags: live-preview, palette-on-ui
- preview a palette live on a mock UI (text/bg/accent) before committing

### [Tints.dev](https://www.tints.dev/)
- tags: tailwind-scale, shade-generator
- generate a Tailwind-style 50-950 shade ramp from one base color
