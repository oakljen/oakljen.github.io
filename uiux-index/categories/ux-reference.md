# ux-reference

### [CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/)
- tags: css, reference, snippets
- long-standing CSS technique reference and snippet archive (Guide to Flexbox/Grid, almanac)

### [Growth.design](https://growth.design/)
- tags: case-study, comic-format, behavioral-design
- comic-style case studies breaking down behavioral-design decisions in real shipped products

- tags: courses, glossary, foundational
- foundational UX courses + glossary, good for grounding terminology and process

- tags: principles, cheatsheet, psychology
- named UX/psychology principles (Fitts's Law, Hick's Law, Jakob's Law...) as a quick-reference cheatsheet

### [Nielsen Norman Group](https://www.nngroup.com/)
- tags: research, usability, authoritative
- the authoritative UX research body — usability heuristics, research-backed pattern guidance

### [Refactoring UI](https://www.refactoringui.com/)
- tags: book, practical-tips, visual-design
- Adam Wathan & Steve Schoger's practical visual-design tips (spacing, contrast, hierarchy) for developers without a design background

### [Smashing Magazine](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/)
- tags: articles, css, ux, front-end
- long-running deep-dive articles on UX, CSS techniques, and front-end craft

### [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/)
- tags: articles, case-studies, medium
