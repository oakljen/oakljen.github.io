# inspiration-gallery

### [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/)
- tags: awards, agency, experimental, motion
- the long-running award-site benchmark, best for experimental/agency-tier craft

### [Behance](https://www.behance.net/)
- tags: case-study, branding, ux-process
- full case studies incl. process/branding, good for narrative and system-level thinking

### [CollectUI](https://collectui.com/)
- tags: daily-ui, component-shots
- Daily UI challenge submissions, good for isolated component ideas

### [Curated.design](https://curated.design/)
- tags: tools, resources, aggregator
- aggregator of design tools and resources across categories

### [Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/)
- tags: shots, concept, visual-design
- concept-level visual design shots, not shipped code — inspiration only, expect over-polish vs reality

### [Footer Design](https://footer.design/)
- tags: footer, section-pattern
- footer-specific pattern gallery

### [Godly](https://www.godly.website/)
- tags: landing-page, interactions, awwwards-tier
- hand-picked best-of-the-web landing pages, strong on interaction/motion

### [Httpster](https://httpster.net/)
- tags: web-design, gallery
- long-running curated web design gallery

### [Land-book](https://land-book.com/)
- tags: landing-page, screenshots, saas, filter-by-industry
- curated landing page screenshots, filterable by industry/style/color

### [Landingfolio](https://www.landingfolio.com/)
- tags: landing-page, by-section, saas
- landing pages broken down by section (hero, pricing, footer...) for pattern reuse

### [Lapa Ninja](https://www.lapa.ninja)
- tags: landing-page, by-industry, by-style
- breadth-of-craft landing page gallery, minimal to maximal, categorized by industry/style

### [Minimal Gallery](https://minimal.gallery/)
- tags: minimal, clean
- minimalist web design showcase

### [Mobbin](https://www.mobbin.com/)
- tags: mobile, app-ui, flows, screenshots
- real shipped mobile & web app screens organized by flow (onboarding, checkout, settings...)

### [Muzli](https://muz.li/)
- tags: feed, aggregator, dribbble, behance
- design feed aggregating Dribbble/Behance/Awwwards/blogs into one stream

### [One Page Love](https://onepagelove.com/)
- tags: one-page, landing-page
- single-page site gallery, good for landing/launch patterns

### [Page Flows](https://pageflows.com/)
- tags: flows, video, onboarding, checkout
- screen-recorded real product flows (onboarding, checkout, signup) — the flow equivalent of Mobbin

### [Screenlane](https://screenlane.com/)
- tags: mobile, web, screenshots
- curated app/web screenshots by category

### [Siteinspire](https://www.siteinspire.com/)
- tags: portfolio, agency, minimal
- curated showcase, strong filter by type/style/subject

### [UI Garage](https://uigarage.net/)
- tags: ui-patterns, mobile, web
- pattern-level UI screenshots (specific components, not full pages)

### [UI Movement](https://uimovement.com/)
- tags: motion, micro-interaction, gif
- micro-interaction and motion pattern gallery (gifs of real UI motion)
